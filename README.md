## Install

1. Minecraft **1.21.11** with **Fabric Loader 0.17.3** or newer (https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/).
2. **Fabric API** for 1.21.11 (`0.141.6+1.21.11` or newer) in your `mods` folder.
3. This jar in your `mods` folder (VFX Weaver is inside it).

The mod is required to join a Souls SMP server. Replace the old jar when a new one is published.

## The GLaDOS voice (optional add-on)

**[https://github.com/ChargeyXD/SoulsSMP/raw/download/SoulsSMP-GLaDOS-Voice.jar](https://github.com/ChargeyXD/SoulsSMP/raw/download/SoulsSMP-GLaDOS-Voice.jar)** - 24.1 MB, md5 `7fb97e4675926e83b85ae3d4607a8896`.

The Soul's Whisper can speak as GLaDOS. Her voice is a separate jar, so nobody has to download it who does not want it:
put it in your `mods` folder beside the mod, then pick GLaDOS in `/soulwhisper`. Update it together with the mod - lines
newer than your add-on speak in English.
